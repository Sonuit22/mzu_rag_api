University Description

About Mizoram University:
Mizoram University is a Central University established in 2001 in Aizawl, Mizoram.
It offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across various academic disciplines with a focus on research, innovation, and higher education excellence.

Motto: “Greater Deeds Remain”
